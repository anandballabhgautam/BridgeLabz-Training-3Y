The Art of Readable Text

A short article on typography

Good typography makes reading easy and enjoyable. The choice of font, the size of the letters and the space between lines all affect how comfortable a page feels. A well-designed page lets readers focus on the message instead of the layout.

Fonts and Weights

Serif fonts such as Georgia are often used for long articles, while sans-serif fonts like Arial look clean on screens. A monospace font is best for code, for example font-family: monospace;. Weight can range from light to extra bold.

Spacing and Alignment

Line-height adds space between lines, letter-spacing adds space between characters and text-align controls how the text lines up. Together, they make a paragraph clear and balanced for the reader.
