Discover Mathura

History Culture Food Visit

History

Mathura is one of the oldest cities in India and is known as the birthplace of Lord Krishna. It has been an important centre of art, trade and religion for thousands of years.

Culture & Festivals

Holi and Janmashtami are celebrated here on a grand scale. Visitors from all over the world come to experience the music, colours and devotion of the city.

Famous Food

Mathura is famous for its peda, kachori and lassi. Street food near the old bazaars is a must-try for every traveller.

Best Time to Visit

October to March has pleasant weather, making it ideal for walking tours of the ghats and temples.
