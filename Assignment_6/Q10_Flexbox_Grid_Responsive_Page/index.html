The Power of Small Daily Habits

Big achievements often begin with small actions repeated consistently.

Building a new habit does not require a dramatic change overnight. Start with a task that feels manageable, connect it to an existing routine and give yourself time to practise. Small steps reduce the pressure of getting started.

As a routine becomes familiar, it takes less effort to continue. Tracking progress can help you notice improvements and understand which strategies work best for you.

Start Small
